Welcome to the Pokemon Beginner Guide

Pokemon is one of the most popular franchises in the world, with hundreds of unique Pokemon to discover. While many people recognize famous Pokemon like Pikachu, they may not know exactly how Pokemon types, abilities, stats, and evolution actually work. Learning these basic features can help beginners understand why Pokemon have different strengths and how they are used in battles.

This beginner guide covers the fundamentals of Pokemon: basic Pokemon information, type matchups, and evolution mechanics. Start with the Pokemon Guide to learn about individual Pokemon, visit the Type Guide to understand strengths and weaknesses, and use the Evolution Guide to learn how Pokemon can change into new forms.
